The Problem We Solve

The World's Most Avoidable Hiring Failure

Employers can't find qualified candidates. Graduates can't find jobs. Universities produce talent that disappears into unemployment. Everyone loses — and it doesn't have to be this way.

No verified student data

Employers have no reliable way to assess academic performance. CVs are unverified and unstandardised.

No targeted discovery

Students post on job boards and hope. Employers sift thousands of mismatched applications with no quality filter.

Slow, expensive hiring cycles

Average time-to-hire for junior roles is 45–90 days. GradLink cuts that to days with pre-verified talent pools.

The Process

Simple on the Surface. Powerful Underneath.

Two distinct journeys. One shared platform. Built for both sides of the equation.

Step 01

Build Your Verified Profile

Upload your university acceptance letter, transcripts, and academic records. Our verification system confirms your credentials so employers know exactly what they're getting.

Step 02

Generate Your Standardised CV

GradLink's CV builder creates a consistent profile every employer reads the same way. The format is locked — what matters is your results, not your design skills.

Step 03

Get Found & Get Hired

Employers actively search GradLink for candidates matching their needs. You don't apply — you're discovered. When there's a match, you receive a direct opportunity from a verified scout.

Step 01

Create Your Scout Profile

Set up your company profile and define your commitment to student talent — internships, part-time roles, or junior positions — to unlock the full verified student database.

Step 02

Search & Filter by What Matters

Filter students by degree, GPA, university, year of study, skills, and availability. Save candidates to shortlists and build talent pipelines long before you need to hire.

Step 03

Reach Out & Recruit Directly

Send opportunities directly to matched students through GradLink's messaging system. Post to the marketplace, or headhunt specific profiles directly — no intermediaries.
